# RapidRoom Styles v1

These recipes are original RapidRoom designs, authored as native adjustments for issue #308. Private photographic references and third-party preset collections were studied to understand colour-separation techniques and the interaction of HSL, channel curves and calibration. No reference preset was converted, distributed or used as the source of a recipe. Names describe the intended look; none claims to reproduce a commercial preset or film stock.

The pack combines shaped luma and RGB channel curves, selective HSL palettes, colour calibration, grading and restrained finishing. Night looks open street detail while separating coloured light, including bold teal/orange and blue/magenta cyberpunk palettes. My Look adds a warm everyday starting point. Daylight looks range from vivid travel colour and turquoise water to luminous pastels, cream architecture, amber texture and lush emerald woodland, vermilion maples, botanical colour, mahogany interiors and bright food. Recipes preserve exposure, camera white balance, crop, lens selection, sharpening and noise reduction. Effect panels owned by a style are enabled on application. Dark Forest adds a soft, editable warm beam using an original radial mask template; unit image coordinates are converted to pixel geometry when applied. Existing masks are preserved, and the added beam fades through mask opacity with Amount.

Amount blends from the edit before application, preserves a coloured grading wheel's hue when fading from neutral, and follows the shortest hue arc between coloured wheels. Active and saved point curves agree. Stable IDs preserve favourites and moved presets; the initial pack migration is recorded so deleting Styles remains a persistent user choice. Fresh libraries contain this pack instead of the previous stock presets. On upgrade, only unchanged stock items still in their original groups are retired; favourites and modified, renamed, moved or imported presets are preserved.

Owner photographs, reference material and taste galleries remain private and outside this repository. Public evidence uses CC0 images only. Taste acceptance and native rendering validation are required before shipping.
