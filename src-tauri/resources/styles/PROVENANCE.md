# RapidRoom Styles v1

These ten recipes are original RapidRoom designs, authored as native adjustments for issue #308. Private photographic references and third-party preset collections were studied to understand colour-separation techniques and the interaction of HSL, channel curves and calibration. No reference preset was converted, distributed or used as the source of a recipe. Names describe the intended look; none claims to reproduce a commercial preset or film stock.

The pack combines shaped luma and RGB channel curves, selective HSL palettes, colour calibration, grading and restrained finishing. Night looks open street detail while separating coloured light. Film looks use different highlight shoulders, shadow colours and palette choices. Monochrome uses colour-channel luminance for separation. Recipes preserve exposure, camera white balance, crop, lens selection, sharpening and noise reduction. Effect panels owned by a style are enabled on application.

Amount blends from the edit before application, preserves a coloured grading wheel's hue when fading from neutral, and follows the shortest hue arc between coloured wheels. Active and saved point curves agree. Stable IDs preserve favourites and moved presets; the initial pack migration is recorded so deleting Styles remains a persistent user choice.

Owner photographs, reference material and taste galleries remain private and outside this repository. Public evidence uses CC0 images only. Taste acceptance and native rendering validation are required before shipping.
