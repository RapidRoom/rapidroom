## Policy implication

This sample supports keeping the proposed gate for ordinary and sync PRs; it
does not justify an automatic sync exemption. A future upstream change that
adds errors, or renames a file with errors, will still fail this implementation.
The existing fixture deliberately verifies that moved debt fails.

If that happens, hold the sync and ask the maintainer to choose a narrowly
reviewed exception or an upstream fix. Do not create RapidRoom-only lint cleanup
in upstream-owned files merely to unblock the sync. A future exception mechanism
should identify the exact base/head, paths/rules/counts (or a verified rename),
prove the increase came from the upstream delta, and retain parser/configuration
failure checks and normal enforcement for RapidRoom edits. Such a mechanism is
not implemented or authorized by this PR; a label alone does not bypass the gate.

Restricting enforcement to whole files RapidRoom changed relative to upstream
would hide new integration errors in other files and needs a separate ownership
policy. The five passing replays provide no evidence requiring that weaker gate.
